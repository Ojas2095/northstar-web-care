## 2. One-Time Paid Pilot ($39 USD)

Before committing to a monthly retainer, skeptical founders can test our execution speed and reliability through an isolated, low-risk paid pilot:

- **Investment:** $39 USD (Fixed Fee)
- **Scope:** Isolate and resolve 1 reported high-priority bug (e.g. mobile navbar layout break, broken webhook submission, or dead links).
- **Turnaround:** 24–48 hours from code access.
- **Guarantee:** 100% money-back guarantee if not resolved.
- **Conversion Upsell:** The $39 fee can be credited toward the first month of Growth Care ($49) or Technical Partner ($99).

## 3. Financial Margin & Capacity Analysis

Assuming a solo technical founder working 10–15 hours weekly:

| Tier | Price | Monthly Time Expended | Effective Hourly Rate | Target Accounts |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Essential Care** | $19 | ~45 mins | ~$25 / hr | 4 clients ($76/mo) |
| **Growth Care** | $49 | ~2.5 hrs | ~$20 / hr | 3 clients ($147/mo) |
| **Technical Partner** | $99 | ~5.5 hrs | ~$18 / hr | 1 client ($99/mo) |

**Blended Capacity:** 8 clients = **$322 USD / month (~₹27,000 INR)**, exceeding the initial business goal of ₹5,000–₹10,000 with under 20 total monthly hours.

## 4. Scope Limitations & Add-On Services

### Non-Negotiable Boundaries
1. **Hosting & Domains:** We configure and deploy, but client retains hosting billing and domain ownership.
2. **Major Redesigns:** Tasks exceeding plan hours are quoted separately as a dedicated project sprint ($35/hr or fixed sprint fee).
3. **Emergency Support:** Support is delivered during business hours (Monday–Friday, 9 AM – 6 PM).
